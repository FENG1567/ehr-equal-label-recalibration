# Model configurations and endpoint qualifications

The checkpoint metadata describe model dimensions, training settings, and the limits of historical pretraining provenance. Exact historical training membership could not be verified. These limitations are also described in the manuscript.

Only the stated hospital-death, secondary ICU-death and same-window decoding outcomes support the reported analyses. Acute kidney injury, length of stay and vasopressor outcomes were not validated as additional future clinical prediction tasks.

The larger configurations differ in training exposure and cannot isolate a causal effect of model scale. Supplementary comparisons retain their original input versions and endpoints.
